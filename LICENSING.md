# Selected software license and attribution

Decision recorded16 October2026: the author delegated selection of a license
for owned code permitting noncommercial use with Luay Kelani credit, modification
and redistribution. Selected **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**;
SPDX identifier `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`.

LICENSE.md incorporates the official standard terms by version-specific URL,
not custom license wording. NOTICE supplies the actual Required Notice crediting
Luay Kelani. Distributors must pass on the license text or its URL and the Required
Notice. This is notice-based attribution, not an obligation to cite an invented
DOI in every paper or a guarantee of visible credit for every private use.

## Permission boundary

Permits noncommercial use and making/distributing modifications or new works
for permitted purposes. **Read the actual definition:** the license expressly
permits use by charitable, educational, public-research, public-safety/health,
environmental and government institutions regardless of funding or resulting
funding obligations. It is not a universal ban on every activity involving money,
nor does it waive statutory fair-use rights. Do not paraphrase it as one.

Commercial activity outside the license's permitted purposes is not granted by
this license. No separate commercial license is offered or authorized here.
Describe the code as noncommercial/source-available, not as unrestricted
permissive open-source software. No MIT or inherited dataset license is applied.

## Covered material and remaining release checks

Applies only to original project code and associated hand-authored software
configuration/instructions Luay owns and may license, on the reported rights
clarification. No blanket legal ownership claim over the entire workspace.
Third-party code/dependencies, models, datasets, templates and other material
keep their own licenses; retain all applicable upstream copyright notices.
Do not overwrite them with a Luay-only notice or relicense material without rights.
Review per-file provenance/third-party compatibility before actual public release;
the author's license choice itself is resolved, not awaiting another selection.

The article, supplement and experimental assets are separate, excluded from this
code-only package and NOT licensed by this software decision. Ramin Abbaszadi's
manuscript coauthorship is unchanged. This does not select a publication license
for the article. Luay subsequently chose to publish the code himself;the real
repository and reserved software DOI are documented in README.md. That choice
does not expand ownership or third-party rights. Scoped import/input/preparation
and file checks have passed;full fit/clean-install proof remains unverified.
The Zenodo draft is not yet published at preparation of this snapshot. See
THIRD_PARTY.md for the provenance and rights-review boundary.
